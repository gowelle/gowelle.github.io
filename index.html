The app I built because my Mac refused to retire

Paddock is a local PHP and Laravel environment for Intel Macs that works the moment it is installed: a native menu bar app, a background service and a command-line tool, written in Swift.

  • Every project folder is served at its own .test address, with trusted HTTPS
  • PHP, databases, a mail catcher and Xdebug are built in
  • Three windows show your app's mail, logs and dumps
  • One command installs it, and it updates itself
